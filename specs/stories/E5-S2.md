# E5-S2: Funnel, manual-review backlog, auto-approval and rejection-reason metrics are computed

- **Epic:** E5 - Reports and Admin Console (Sprint 5)
- **Layer:** Service
- **Group:** J
- **depends_on:** E4-S1, E4-S3
- **Covers:** AC-10, AC-07

## Description

Metrics that depend on decisions and overrides: the approval funnel, backlog, auto-approval rate against the proposed 6000 bp (60 percent) target, and the top rejection reasons.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The approval funnel returns, for INITIATED, DOCS_SUBMITTED, SCREENED, CLASSIFIED and each final outcome, the number of cases that ever reached it and the conversion from the previous stage in integer basis points.
2. The manual-review backlog returns the current MANUAL_REVIEW count, oldest age in minutes and counts in buckets under 60 minutes, 60 to 1440 minutes and over 1440 minutes.
3. Top rejection reasons returns the five most frequent reason_codes among REJECTED cases (from override rows), ordered by count descending then code ascending, with counts.
4. Auto-approval rate is AUTO decisions with outcome APPROVED divided by all decided cases in basis points, returned with the target 6000 and a met flag.
5. Every metric honours the product and from/to filters.
