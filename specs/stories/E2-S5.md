# E2-S5: Prospect portal lets the applicant register, upload and track status

- **Epic:** E2 - Document Upload and Classification Stub (Sprint 2)
- **Layer:** UI
- **Group:** F
- **depends_on:** E2-S2, E2-S4, E1-S3
- **Covers:** AC-01, AC-02, AC-09

## Description

A responsive, accessible prospect portal covers lead registration, profile, checklist upload with per-item status, submission and status tracking with re-upload.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The lead form validates name, contact and product on the client with inline error text linked by aria-describedby, and on success shows the case_id.
2. The upload page lists the product checklist with a status chip per item (Missing, Uploaded, Verified, Flagged, Rejected) and the Submit button stays disabled until every mandatory item has a document.
3. The status page shows the current state and an action-required list; each entry has a Re-upload control that uploads a replacement and refreshes the chip without navigating away from the case.
4. At viewport widths 360 px and 1280 px no page has horizontal scrolling and all controls remain reachable.
5. Each prospect page has zero serious or critical axe-core violations, every control is keyboard operable, and text contrast is at least 4.5:1 (WCAG 2.1 AA).
