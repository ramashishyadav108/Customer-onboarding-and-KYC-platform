# E4-S5: Compliance review queue and portal notifications are available in the UI

- **Epic:** E4 - Manual Review, Decisions and Account Creation (Sprint 4)
- **Layer:** UI
- **Group:** J
- **depends_on:** E4-S3, E4-S4, E4-S1, E2-S5
- **Covers:** AC-08, AC-09

## Description

The compliance officer works the review queue in the browser. The prospect portal status page shows the notification history.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The review queue page lists MANUAL_REVIEW cases oldest first with reason code, product and age, and is reachable only by compliance-officer.
2. The review panel shows documents, screening hits and risk breakdown, and the Approve and Reject buttons stay disabled until a reason code is selected.
3. After an override the case disappears from the queue, and an APPROVE shows the new account number in a confirmation message.
4. The prospect status page lists notifications newest first with their event text.
5. The decision form traps and restores focus (dialog role, Escape closes) and both pages have zero serious or critical axe-core violations.
