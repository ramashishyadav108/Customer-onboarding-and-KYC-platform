# E4-S1: Low-risk clean cases are auto-approved and all others routed to manual review

- **Epic:** E4 - Manual Review, Decisions and Account Creation (Sprint 4)
- **Layer:** Service
- **Group:** H
- **depends_on:** E3-S3, E4-S2
- **Covers:** AC-07, AC-05, AC-04, NFR-02, NFR-08

## Description

The decision step runs on a CLASSIFIED case. LOW risk, no AML/PEP hit and all current documents VERIFIED means APPROVED with stubbed account creation. Everything else becomes MANUAL_REVIEW with a reason code. The step is idempotent and approved cases become immutable.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. POST /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/decide on a CLASSIFIED case with band LOW, no screening hit and every current document VERIFIED moves the case to APPROVED, records a decision (type AUTO, reason_code AUTO_APPROVED, rule_version) and creates the stub account.
2. Routing to MANUAL_REVIEW with reason code, in precedence order: screening hit gives AML_HIT or PEP_HIT, any current document not VERIFIED gives DOC_UNRECOGNISED, band MEDIUM gives RISK_MEDIUM, band HIGH gives RISK_HIGH; none of these create an account.
3. Once a case is APPROVED, any attempt to change its documents, profile, risk assessment or state raises CaseLockedError (API 409 CASE_LOCKED) and changes nothing.
4. Decisions are appended to an append-only table (case_id, type, outcome, reason_code, rule_version, actor, UTC timestamp); calling decide twice returns the original decision without a second row.
5. POST /api/v1/cases/{case_id}/advance runs the pending steps in order (screen, classify, decide) on a DOCS_SUBMITTED case and returns the final state; calling it again returns the same state without new history rows.
